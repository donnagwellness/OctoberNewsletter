In the Kitchen

Croatian Blitva

A simple Dalmatian side, olive oil, greens and garlic

Picked this one up along the Croatian coast, it's the classic side dish you'll find next to grilled fish all along the Dalmatian coastline. Simple, a little garlicky, and genuinely good for you, swiss chard, potato and good olive oil.

4 servings ~140 cal / serve

Ingredients

  • 1 large bunch Swiss chard (silverbeet), stems and leaves separated
  • 3 medium potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 4 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Lemon wedges, to serve

Method

  1. Boil the potatoes in salted water until just tender, about 12 minutes.
  2. Add the chopped chard stems and leaves to the same pot for the last 3-4 minutes, then drain everything together well.
  3. Warm the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat and gently fry the garlic until fragrant, not browned.
  4. Add the drained potato and chard, season with salt and pepper, and toss gently to coat in the garlicky oil.
  5. Serve warm with a good squeeze of lemon.

Donna's Tip: don't skimp on the olive oil, it's what carries all the flavour here, and a good quality extra virgin makes all the difference.
